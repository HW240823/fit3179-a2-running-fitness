Running and Fitness in Australia

How do Australians participate in running and fitness, how does participation vary across states, and where are marathon events held?

1. Participation patterns

Compare participation across activities, years, gender and age. Together, these charts show how popular each activity is and how participation differs between groups.

Fitness/Gym vs Running

In 2025, overall participation was higher for Fitness/Gym (34.1%) than for Running (16.5%). The 2024–2025 comparison also shows a larger increase for Fitness/Gym than for Running.

Gender gap in running

The male–female gap in running participation is about 4.8 percentage points in 2025.

Participation by age

This chart compares running and fitness participation rates across age groups in 2025, so you can see how each activity changes with age.

Running participation by state

States and territories ranked by their running participation rate in 2025.

Rate versus number of runners

Participation rate shows the share of people participating; estimated participant counts show the number of people. These measures describe different things.

States with similar profiles

The dendrogram groups states by their standardised running and fitness rates. Victoria and Western Australia form a close pair, as do New South Wales and South Australia.
