1. Participation patterns

Compare participation across activities, years, gender and age. Together, these charts show how popular each activity is and how participation differs between groups.

In 2025, overall participation was higher for Fitness/Gym (34.1%) than for Running (16.5%). The 2024–2025 comparison also shows a larger increase for Fitness/Gym than for Running.

2. Where running participation happens

The maps compare the geographic pattern of running participation across Australian states. Participation rate shows the share of people participating; estimated participant counts show the number of people. These measures describe different things.

The dendrogram groups states by their standardised running and fitness rates. Victoria and Western Australia form a close pair, as do New South Wales and South Australia.

3. Marathon events and finishes

These charts examine marathon events by state and month, then show how total finishes change across years.

Data and chart specifications are available in the project repository. Participation figures shown here are for Australia, 2025, unless otherwise indicated in a chart.
